Marity Trade • Client Data Privacy

Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: November 2026 • Compliant with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1 & Google Play Developer Policy

1. Data Collection & Usage

Marity Trade collects only essential customer data required to fulfill luxury fragrance orders and maintain customer account records:

  • Contact Information: Full name, delivery address, phone number, and email address for order fulfillment and dispatch notification.
  • Order Information: Curated fragrance selection, bottle sizes, custom gold cap engraving initials, and transaction receipts.
  • Account Security: One-time 4-digit verification codes sent via SMS/Email to secure access to delivery tracking.

2. Physical Goods Exemption

All items sold via Marity Trade are physical luxury goods (flacons, pure extraits, case allocations, and coffrets) dispatched via White Glove Express courier. In accordance with platform guidelines, physical merchandise purchases are settled securely via licensed payment gateways and mobile money providers.

3. In-App Account & Data Deletion

Every customer reserves the absolute right to purge all personal identification data and customer records. You may trigger account deletion directly inside the application via the Client Privilège → Request Account & Data Deletion control, or by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@maritytrade.online.

4. Delivery & Customer Concierge

All orders are hand-inspected and dispatched with verified arrival within 12 to 24 hours across Accra and priority regional zones. For inquiries, contact the Marity Trade Boutique Concierge at info@maritytrade.online.
