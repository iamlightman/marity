# Marity Fragrances — Haute Parfumerie Mobile Application

A realistic, premium, production-grade mobile e-commerce application inspired by high-end international luxury fragrance houses and advanced liquid metallic glass material design.

---

## ✦ Design Direction & Material System

1. **Liquid Metallic Glass Architecture**
   - **Pearl-Glass Surfaces**: Product cards with subtle pearl-white luminance, multi-layered occlusion shadows, and authentic micro-bevel reflections.
   - **Translucent Frosted Glass**: Header and bottom floating navigation dock featuring `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(190%)` and 1px platinum highlight borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
   - **Metallic Specular Chamfers**: Floating pill buttons with deep charcoal cores, platinum specular highlight lines, and champagne gold typography.
   - **Fluid Platinum Canvas**: Background with subtle platinum wave ripples, silk curves, and ambient light refractions.

2. **Colour Palette**
   - **Pearl White**: `#FAF9F6`, `#FFFFFF`
   - **Soft Silver & Platinum**: `#E2E8F0`, `#E8ECF2`
   - **Champagne Gold**: `#D4AF37`, `#E5C378`, `#B8891B`
   - **Smoky Transparent Glass**: `rgba(20, 24, 33, 0.45)`
   - **Deep Charcoal Accent**: `#11151F`, `#181E2B`

---

## ✦ Key Features & Native App Capabilities

### 1. Interactive Device Frame & Viewport Controls
- **Native iPhone 16 Pro Luxury Titanium Frame**: Includes Dynamic Island (displays live shopping bag totals and status), iOS status bar (9:41, cellular, Wi-Fi, battery 100%), home indicator, and glass gloss glare.
- **Fullscreen / Responsive Toggle**: Instantly switch between the iPhone mockup shell and full-bleed mobile viewport for testing on actual physical smartphones.
- **Luxury Tactile Acoustic Engine (Web Audio API)**: Procedurally synthesizes crystal glass chimes (add-to-bag), soft metallic clicks (button interactions), fine perfume atomizer spray mist puffs, and regal checkout success chords (toggleable on/off).

### 2. Comprehensive Haute Parfumerie Catalog
- **The Art of Fragrance (Grand Cru Éternel)** — Extrait de Parfum (Persian saffron, aged golden amber, Laotian oud).
- **Ocean Breeze (L'Onde Azurée)** — Eau de Parfum (Calabrian bergamot, mineral sea salt, gray ambergris).
- **Velvet Bloom (Éclat de Soie)** — Eau de Parfum (Grasse Centifolia rose, Madagascar pink pepper, Tuscan orris butter).
- **Obsidian Noir (Sillage Ténébreux)** — Extrait de Parfum (Smoked Cambodian oud, Tuscan glove leather, black cardamom).
- **Soleil d'Or (Nectar Solaire)** — Eau de Parfum (Sicilian neroli, orange blossom honey, solar amber).
- **Santal Blanc (Pur Velours)** — Eau de Parfum (Australian white sandalwood, green cardamom, clean musks).
- **Fleur Sauvage (Émeraude Botanique)** — Eau de Parfum (Wild galbanum, Indian tuberose, green vetiver).
- **Discovery Coffret Prestige** — Handcrafted black lacquer box with 5 luxury 10ml travel flacons.

### 3. Product Details Experience
- **Multi-Angle Gallery**: High-resolution studio photography with interactive thumbnail selector.
- **Dynamic Flacon Volume Selector**: 50ml, 100ml, 200ml with live price and volume calculation.
- **Olfactory Pyramid**: Top, Heart, and Base notes with dedicated note chips.
- **Longevity & Sillage Meters**: Skin longevity, sillage projection rating, and season profile.
- **Complimentary Diamond-Tool Flacon Engraving**: Live interactive custom monogram input (e.g. `J.L.`) dynamically rendered onto the golden flacon cap!

### 4. Full Functional E-Commerce Engine
- **Live Search Overlay**: Instant search matching fragrance names, olfactory notes, or concentrations.
- **Category Navigation**: Men's, Women's, Unisex, Gifts, and All Signatures with active glass pod states.
- **Persistent Wishlist**: Animated heart toggle, dedicated Wishlist view, and "Add All to Bag".
- **Slide-Over Shopping Bag Drawer**:
  - Quantity management (+ / -) with instant price calculations.
  - Free Worldwide Express Shipping progress bar ($150 target).
  - Complimentary 2ml luxury sample picker (choose up to 2).
  - Signature gift packaging toggle with custom handwritten card note.
  - Promo code verification (`MARITY10` for 10% off, `GOLDEN` for $20 off, `VIPCONCIERGE` for 15% off).
- **Multi-Step Luxury Checkout**:
  - Step 1: Destination address validation.
  - Step 2: Courier selection (DHL Express Worldwide vs White Glove Concierge).
  - Step 3: Payment settlement (One-Tap Apple Pay with FaceID biometric animation or Client Credit Card).
  - Step 4: Grand Flacon confirmation receipt with generated order reference (`#MR-2026-XXXX`).
- **Orders & Live Concierge Tracking**:
  - Step-by-step interactive DHL timeline: Order Authenticated ➔ Custom Engraving at Grasse Atelier ➔ White Glove Quality Passed ➔ Dispatched via DHL Express Worldwide ➔ Out for Delivery.
  - Tracking number with one-click copy and digital receipt view.
- **Account & VIP Lounge**:
  - Platinum Connoisseur metallic member card with foil shine.
  - Loyalty points balance (1,450 pts) and milestone progress bar.
  - Olfactory Diagnostic (Interactive 3-question Scent Finder Quiz).
